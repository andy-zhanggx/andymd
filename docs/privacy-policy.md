# AndyMD Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 September 2026_

AndyMD does not collect any data.

## What AndyMD stores

AndyMD is a local Markdown editor. It reads and writes files in folders **you**
choose through the macOS open panel, and it stores its own settings — your
preferences, recent folders, open tabs and version history — inside the app's
own container on your Mac.

macOS grants AndyMD access only to the folders you pick. That access is
remembered between launches using a standard macOS mechanism called a
security-scoped bookmark, so the app can reopen the folder you were last working
in. It cannot reach anything you have not chosen.

## What AndyMD sends

Nothing. AndyMD makes no network requests. It has no analytics, no crash
reporting, no telemetry, no advertising and no accounts. Your documents never
leave your Mac.

The app declares the `com.apple.security.network.client` entitlement because the
system web view AndyMD is built on will not start without it. The app itself
does not use it to contact anything.

## Third parties

There are none. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

## Children

AndyMD is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including
children.
